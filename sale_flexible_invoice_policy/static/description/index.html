Flexible Invoice Policy

------

Invoice immediately when exceptions arise, without changing your product settings.

One simple toggle. Full control. Complete audit trail.

What We're Solving

----------------

The Exception Problem

Customer needs invoice NOW for accounting, but you use delivery-based invoicing. Changing product settings affects ALL orders, not just this one.

Manual Workarounds

Creating manual invoices or temporarily changing configurations is error-prone, time-consuming, and risky.

No Permission Control

Anyone with invoice rights can bypass your workflow. No audit trail, no control, no consistency.

How It Works

A simple toggle that gives you control when you need it, without breaking your workflow.

One-Click Exception

Enable the toggle on any sales order to invoice the full amount immediately, regardless of delivery status.

Permission Protected

Only authorized users (Sales/Finance Managers) can enable exceptions. Everyone else won't even see the toggle.

Complete Audit Trail

Every change is logged in the order's activity feed with username and timestamp. Perfect for compliance.

Auto-Reset

Toggle automatically turns off after invoicing, so each exception is intentional. No accidental reuse.

Easy Setup

----------------

1

Install

Go to Apps → Search "Flexible Invoice Policy" → Click Install. That's it!

2

Set Permissions

Settings → Users → Enable "Allow Invoice Without Delivery" for managers who should have this power.

3

Start Using

Authorized users will see the toggle in sales orders. Enable when needed, invoice immediately!

Pro Tip: The toggle auto-resets after invoicing by default. To keep it enabled, adjust the system parameter in Settings → Technical → System Parameters.

How to Use

----------------

Normal Flow (Wait for Delivery)
  1. Create and confirm your sales order
  2. Ship the products
  3. Click "Create Invoice"
  4. Invoice only what was delivered ✓
Exception Flow (Invoice Now!)
  1. Create and confirm your sales order
  2. Enable the toggle in the order header
  3. Click "Create Invoice"
  4. Invoice the full amount immediately ✓
Security & Compliance
  • Every toggle change is logged with user and timestamp
  • Only authorized managers can enable exceptions
  • Auto-reset prevents accidental misuse
  • Full audit trail for compliance reviews
