Technical Specification for AI & LLM

This Odoo module 'stock_landed_cost_variance' records, per stock.move, the part of a later revaluation that belongs to goods already gone. It creates the model 'stock.value.variance' with the fields base_amount, capitalized_amount and expensed_amount, captured after stock.landed.cost.button_validate and after account.move._post for vendor bills that re-value a receipt. The split is derived by calling product._run_fifo_get_stack() directly, never move.remaining_qty, because the native remaining dictionary mixes units of measure. It posts one aggregated reclassification entry per company, grouped by origin and product category, using three accounts declared on the product category. It creates no stock move and never rewrites the value of a done move. Compatible with Odoo 19 (Enterprise, Odoo.SH, Ganemo Online). Multi-language support for English and Spanish included.

Value Variance

Late Landed Costs & Bills, Split and Traceable

When a landed cost or a vendor bill revalues a receipt after part of the goods have already left, this module records exactly how much of that amount belongs to what was sold — and turns it into a journal entry you can substantiate line by line.

What We're Solving

----------------

The Two Halves Never Match

Odoo 19 adds a landed cost to the movement in full, but capitalises only the part still in stock — and posts nothing at all when nothing is left. The stock ledger and the accounts drift apart by the difference, permanently, on every import.

Nobody Can Point At It

The amount attributable to the goods already sold stays in the freight expense account, unidentified: no line ties it to the product, the shipment or the sale. This module gives it a record, a number and an entry.

Setup & User Manual

Step-by-Step implementation

1. Configuration Guide

Three accounts per product category, declared once:

  • Go to Inventory > Configuration > Product Categories.
  • Open the Value Variance Accounts section.
  • Stock Valuation: your inventory account.
  • Inventory Variation: the counterpart for landed costs.
  • Cost of Sales: where the variance is finally recognised.
No silent fallback: if an account is missing, the module says which one and stops. It never picks a native account for you — that is how a reclassification ends up on the wrong side of the P&L.

2. Operating Manual (Workflow)

What happens, and when:

Capture Is Automatic

Validate a landed cost, or post a vendor bill that changes the value of a receipt, and the split is recorded on the spot: how much stayed in stock, how much belongs to goods already gone.

The Entry Is Explicit

Posting is a deliberate action, never a side effect of validating a cost or a bill: a locked period must never block your warehouse. Lock dates are checked before posting.

One Entry, Many Rows

A sweep posts one aggregated entry per company, grouped by origin and category. The variance rows are its supporting schedule: the sum is the amount, each row a line of the working paper, linked both ways.

Adjustable, Then Frozen

An accountant can correct the split before it is posted; the capitalised half follows automatically so the two always add up. Once the entry is posted the amount is locked until that entry is reversed.

Valuation Is Never Touched

No stock move is created, no done move is rewritten, no average cost is recomputed. Uninstall it and your valuation is exactly what native Odoo produced.

Lots and Multi-Company

Lot-valuated products get one row per lot, rebuilt from the same FIFO stack Odoo uses. Every row carries its company, and each company posts its own entry.

QA / User Testing Scenarios

Enterprise Validation Plan

Scenario 1: Landed Cost After a Sale

  1. Receive 5 units at 500 each.
  2. Deliver 3 units.
  3. Create and validate a landed cost of 250 on that receipt.
  4. Test: a variance row appears on the receipt with Capitalised 100 and Belongs to Goods Gone 150.

Scenario 2: Vendor Bill Above the Purchase Order

  1. Order and receive at 500, deliver part of it.
  2. Post the vendor bill at 560.
  3. Test: a variance row of origin Vendor Bill is recorded for the difference, split the same way.
  4. Test: post the bill at exactly the order price. No row is created.

Scenario 3: Nothing Left in Stock

Deliver all the units, then apply the landed cost. Native Odoo posts no entry at all.

  • Capitalised = 0, Belongs to Goods Gone = the whole amount.
  • The reclassification entry recognises it as cost of sales.

Scenario 4: Governance

  1. Post the reclassification entry for a variance.
  2. Try to change Belongs to Goods Gone on that row.
  3. Result: refused, naming the entry to reverse first.
  4. Try Compute again on the validated landed cost.
  5. Result: refused — recomputing would wipe the lines that substantiate a posted entry.

Scenario 5: Accounts and Periods

  1. Leave one of the three accounts empty on the category and post.
  2. Result: a clear error naming the missing account. Nothing is posted against a guessed account.
  3. Set the cost of sales account to an account already used as a stock variation account, and post.
  4. Result: refused — the periodic closing would silently cancel the entry.
  5. Close the period and post again. Result: the lock date is named and nothing is posted.

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Common Resolutions

Does it change my inventory valuation?

No. It records and reclassifies. It creates no stock move and never rewrites the value of a validated movement.

Result: average cost, standard price and total value stay exactly as native Odoo computed them.

No variance row was created. Why?

Reason: the revaluation was zero, all the goods were still in stock, or the movement is pinned by a manual valuation adjustment.

Fix: the pinned case is written to the server log with the movement and the cost, so it is never skipped silently.

Does the entry change my result?

No, it reclassifies. The amount was already in the freight expense account; the entry moves it to cost of sales, where it belongs.

Detail: for a vendor bill under periodic valuation no entry is needed — the closing already gets it right, and the module says so.

Is it tied to a localization?

Answer: No.

Detail: it depends on Landed Costs and Purchase only. The three accounts are yours to declare, so any chart of accounts fits. A companion bridge feeds the figures into a valued stock ledger when you have one.
