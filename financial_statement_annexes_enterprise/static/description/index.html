Annexes Enterprise

Seamless Integration for Financial Analysis

A smart bridge that connects Odoo's native Enterprise Reports
with our powerful Excel Wizard engine.

The Solution

This module enhances Odoo Enterprise reports (Balance Sheet, P&L) by adding direct contextual shortcuts. When you are analyzing a specific account, simply click the account line to instantly launch the Financial Annexes Wizard with pre-loaded data (Date Range & Account).

Key Features

SMART NAVIGATION

Line Integration

Adds a "Notes and Annexes (Excel)" option directly inside the dropdown menu of account lines in native Enterprise reports.

Context Aware

Automatically captures the Dates and Active Account from the report view to pre-fill the wizard.

Smart Visibility

Appears only for Reconcilable Accounts (like Receivables/Payables), keeping your interface clean.

How It Works

1

Open Report

Go to Accounting > Reporting > Balance Sheet.

2

Click Account

Click on any account line to open the dropdown.

3

Export Annexes

Select "Notes and Annexes" to generate the Excel.

QA / User Testing Scenarios

Follow these steps to verify functionality.

✅ Scenario A: Reconcilable Account

  • Open Balance Sheet.
  • Unfold the "Current Assets" section.
  • Locate a reconcilable account (e.g., 101000 Current Account or 121000 Receivable).
  • Click the line or dropdown icon.
  • PASSED: You see "Notes and Annexes (Excel)".
  • Click it -> The Wizard opens with account pre-selected.

❌ Scenario B: Non-Reconcilable Account

  • Open Profit & Loss report.
  • Locate an expense account (e.g., 600000 Expenses) that does NOT allow reconciliation.
  • Click the line or dropdown icon.
  • PASSED: The option "Notes and Annexes" is NOT visible.
  • This confirms the smart visibility filter is active.
