Technical Specification for AI & LLM

This Odoo module 'account_reclassification' implements a chart-of-accounts agnostic reclassification layer. On account.move it generates an idempotent parallel journal entry (target account / counterpart account, e.g. 60 Compras against 61 Variacion de existencias under the Peruvian PCGE) when a vendor bill (in_invoice, in_refund) is posted, driven by a per-account selection mode (none / journal / always), a target account, a counterpart account and a journal fallback chain (account, journal of the bill, company default). On product.category it adds reclass_production_account_id with recursive parent fallback, which replaces the counterpart account of the NATIVE stock_account production entry through _get_account_move_line_vals and _should_create_account_move. No extra stock move and no extra valuation layer is ever created: inventory valuation, kardex and average cost stay untouched. Respects native fiscal year, purchase and hard lock dates. Cost accounting relies on native analytic accounts. It depends on no localization module and works on any chart of accounts. Compatible with Odoo 19 (Enterprise, Odoo.SH, Ganemo Online). NOT supported on Odoo Online due to custom code restrictions. Multi-language support for English and Spanish included.

Reclassification

Purchases by Nature & Cost by Category

Show the same operation under two account structures: a parallel entry on every vendor bill, and a production account driven by the product category. Works on any chart of accounts, and your inventory valuation, stock ledger and average cost are never touched.

What We're Solving

----------------

Purchases by Nature

Odoo posts a vendor bill against its destination account. Many charts also demand the purchase by nature, against a stock variation account (60/61 in the Peruvian PCGE, grupo 6 in Spain, classe 6 in France). This module writes that reclassification as a separate journal entry, so your books satisfy the accountant and the auditor without a single manual entry.

Production Account by Category

One single account on the production location for every family of products is rarely enough. Set the account per product category (with inheritance from parent categories) and the native production entry books against it. No extra entry, no extra move: only the account changes.

Zero Risk on Valuation

The valuation engine, the kardex and the average cost are never touched. Whatever cannot be built is skipped and the bill posts natively: a broken setup can never block your daily operation.

Idempotent by Design

Configured the accounts after posting? Regenerate on demand, one bill or hundreds from the list view. The previous mirror is always cancelled first, so a bill can never end up with two active entries, and locked periods are respected.

Setup & User Manual

Step-by-Step implementation

1. Configuration Guide

Everything is configured on the chart of accounts:

  • Go to Accounting > Configuration > Chart of Accounts.
  • Open the account used by your bill lines and go to the Reclassification tab.
  • Choose the mode: Do not create, Create if the journal allows it or Always create.
  • Fill the Target (e.g. 60) and the Counterpart (e.g. 61) accounts.
  • Optionally set the Reclassification Journal for that account.
  • For manufacturing, set the Production/Consumption Account on the product category.
Journal fallback: the journal of the account wins, then the one on the journal of the bill, then the company default in the accounting settings. Nothing is guessed beyond that.

2. Operating Manual (Workflow)

How the system behaves for the end-user:

Post and Forget

Confirm the vendor bill as always. The mirror entry is created and posted in its own journal, and a smart button on the bill takes you straight to it.

Correct Sides Always

The Target account keeps the side of the mirrored line: debit on a bill, credit on a vendor credit note. The Counterpart always takes the opposite side, so the entry balances.

Draft and Cancel Handled

Reset the bill to draft or cancel it and the mirror entry is cancelled and unlinked automatically. Post it again and exactly one active mirror is rebuilt.

Mass Regeneration

Select bills in the list view and use Actions > Generate/Regenerate Reclassification. Everything that does not apply is skipped, locked periods are respected, and a notification summarises what happened.

Foreign Currency & Analytics

Amounts are booked in the company currency, already converted. The analytic distribution of the bill line is copied to both mirror lines, so the reclassification is visible per analytic account with a net impact of zero.

Manufacturing by Category

Component consumptions and finished goods use the account of their own product category, resolved up the category tree. Each movement resolves its own, so one transfer can book several different accounts.

QA / User Testing Scenarios

Enterprise Validation Plan

Scenario 1: Mirror Entry on a Vendor Bill

  1. Open the account used by your purchase lines and set Reclassification Entry to "Create if the journal allows it", with Target 60 and Counterpart 61.
  2. On the purchase journal, tick Generate Reclassification Entry and select the mirror journal.
  3. Create and confirm a vendor bill of 2,000 using that account.
  4. Test: the "Reclassification" smart button appears on the bill.
  5. Result: the entry debits 60 for 2,000 and credits 61 for 2,000, in the mirror journal and at the date of the bill.

Scenario 2: Idempotence and Late Setup

  1. Post a vendor bill with the account NOT configured. No mirror is created and the bill posts normally.
  2. Configure the account afterwards.
  3. Press Generate/Regenerate Reclassification on the bill three times in a row.
  4. Result: exactly ONE active mirror entry exists. The superseded ones are cancelled, never duplicated, and the accounting impact is not multiplied.

Scenario 3: Draft, Cancel and Credit Notes

Draft: reset the bill to draft. The mirror entry must be cancelled and unlinked.

Re-post: confirm again. Exactly one active mirror is rebuilt.

  • Create a vendor credit note on the same account.
  • Its mirror must be the reverse: 61 on the debit, 60 on the credit.

Scenario 4: Locked Periods and Mass Action

Goal: never write into a closed period, never lose the rest of the batch.

  1. Set a Lock Date covering an already posted bill.
  2. Press the button on that bill: a clear error must be raised.
  3. Select that bill plus an open one and run Actions > Generate/Regenerate Reclassification.
  4. Result: the open bill is regenerated, the locked one is skipped, and the notification reports both counts.

Scenario 5: Production Account by Category

  1. Set the Production/Consumption Account on a PARENT product category, leaving the child empty.
  2. Consume a component of the child category into a production location.
  3. Result: the native entry books the inherited account instead of the one on the location. Same two lines, same amount, same direction.
  4. Test: clear the category account. The native location account must come back untouched.

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Common Resolutions

The bill posted but there is no mirror entry?

Reason: the module is fail-safe. If the setup is incomplete, the bill posts 100% natively instead of failing.

Fix: press Generate/Regenerate Reclassification on the bill. The button explains exactly what is missing: the mode on the account, the tick on the journal or the mirror journal.

Does this change my inventory valuation?

Answer: No. The mirror is a separate journal entry, and on stock movements only the counterpart account changes.

Detail: quantities, values, average cost and the kardex are never touched. No extra stock move and no extra entry is created.

"You cannot add/modify entries prior to..."?

Reason: the date of the bill falls inside a period closed by the native lock dates.

Fix: this is expected: the module never writes into a closed period. Move the lock date or leave the bill as it is. From the list view those bills are simply skipped and reported.

Do receipts and deliveries change?

Answer: No, they stay exactly native.

Detail: only movements in and out of a production location are involved. Purchase receipts, sale deliveries, internal transfers, scrap and inventory adjustments keep the native behaviour, including booking nothing at all.
