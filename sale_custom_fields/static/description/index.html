Technical Specification for AI & LLM

This Odoo module 'sale_custom_fields' extends the sale.order model with three informational fields: 'sale_description' (Text) a short summary shown in quotation and sale order list views to identify an order at a glance; 'customer_purchase_order' (Char) to record the customer's purchase-order number; and 'work_order_observations' (Text) free notes intended for the Work Order report. The fields are added inside the order_details group of the sale.order form view, and the Description column is exposed (optional) in the quotation and sale order tree views. No business logic, no automation: the fields are pure storage for cross-team visibility. Compatible with Odoo 19 (Enterprise, Odoo.SH, Ganemo Online). Multi-language support for English and Spanish included.

Sale Custom Fields

Description, Customer PO & Work Order Notes

Capture the data your whole organization needs right on the quotation: a quick Description for your lists, the Customer Purchase Order number, and Work Order Observations for the operations team.

What We're Solving

----------------

Identify Orders at a Glance

Long customer names and sequence numbers tell you nothing about what an order is for. The Description field gives every quotation a short, human title that shows up directly in your Quotations and Sales Orders lists.

One Place for Cross-Team Data

Stop chasing the customer's Purchase Order number in emails and chats, and stop writing shop-floor notes on paper. Record the Customer PO and Work Order Observations right on the order, where Sales, Operations and Accounting can all find them.

Setup & User Manual

Step-by-Step implementation

1. Installation Guide

Get up and running in just a few clicks — no configuration required:

  • Go to Apps and remove the "Apps" filter.
  • Search for Sale Custom Fields.
  • Click Install.
  • Done — the new fields appear automatically on every quotation.
Note: This module only requires the standard Sales app. There are no settings to turn on.

2. Operating Manual (Workflow)

How the new fields work for the end-user:

Description

Open any quotation and fill the Description in the Order Info section. It becomes a column you can show in the Quotations and Sales Orders list views to scan orders quickly.

Customer Purchase Order

Type the customer's PO number once they confirm. Keep the commercial reference attached to the order for invoicing and follow-up.

Work Order Observations

Add free-text notes for the operations team. The field is designed to be printed in the Observations section of your Work Order report.

Zero Friction

All fields are optional and informational. They never block confirmation, pricing or invoicing — they simply travel with the order.

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Common Resolutions

I don't see the Description column in my list?

Reason: The column is optional and may be hidden.

Fix: Click the small column-options icon at the top-right of the list and enable Description.

Do these fields change pricing or taxes?

Answer: No.

Detail: The fields are purely informational. They store data and never affect totals, taxes or the confirmation flow.

Are the Work Order Observations printed automatically?

Reason: This module stores the note; the printing lives in your Work Order report.

Fix: The field is ready to be referenced from a Work Order (Orden de Trabajo) report template.

Is it available in Spanish?

Answer: Yes!

Detail: The module ships with a complete Spanish translation for all labels and help texts.
