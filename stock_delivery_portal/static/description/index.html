Setup & User Manual

Comprehensive implementation guide

1. Configuration Guide

Follow these steps to prepare your Odoo instance for the Delivery Portal:

A. Delivery Picking Types
  • Go to Inventory > Configuration > Operation Types.
  • Enable Show Items in Portal Delivery to let drivers see contents.
  • Enable Allow Driver Contact to activate WhatsApp/Call buttons.
B. Delivery States
  • Go to Inventory > Configuration > Delivery States.
  • Create states (e.g., In Transit, Delivered).
  • Mark final states as Result State.
  • Toggle Require Photo/Signature per state.
PWA Pro-Tip: Access the portal URL on Chrome/Safari and use "Add to Home Screen" for a full-screen app experience with offline support.

2. Operating Manual (Driver Workflow)

Standard field operation for drivers:

Dashboard Management

Drivers see their assigned deliveries for the day. Use Pull-to-Refresh to check for new assignments and toggle between To Do and History.

Instant Scanner

The bottom Scan Delivery button opens the camera to identify packages. If multiple deliveries match, the list filters automatically; if one matches, it redirects instantly.

Contact & Navigation

One-tap WhatsApp messages and GPS Navigation (Google Maps/Apple Maps/Waze) ensure drivers reach customers faster without typing addresses.

Digital Evidence

The Finalize button triggers the proof-of-delivery screen. Mandatory signatures, photos, and receiver names are captured and saved directly to the Odoo Picking.

QA / User Testing Scenarios

Enterprise Validation Plan

Scenario 1: Real-time Sync & Refresh

  1. Assign a new Picking to a Driver in the Odoo backend.
  2. On the mobile device, perform a Pull-to-Refresh gesture.
  3. Result: The new delivery MUST appear instantly in the "To Do" list without a full browser reload.

Scenario 2: Smart Scan-to-Action

  1. Open the Scan Delivery modal.
  2. Scan a unique barcode of a pending Picking.
  3. Result: The app MUST automatically redirect the driver to the Detail View of that specific picking.
  4. Scan a barcode that matches multiple lines.
  5. Result: The list MUST filter to show only matching deliveries.

Scenario 3: Communication & Navigation

  1. Open a Delivery Detail.
  2. Click the WhatsApp button.
  3. Result: Mobile app MUST open WhatsApp with the customer's number pre-filled.
  4. Click Navigate with Maps.
  5. Result: A selector for Google/Apple Maps/Waze MUST appear and correctly pass coordinates to the chosen app.

Scenario 4: Package & Item Integrity

  1. Enable "Show Items in Portal" in the Picking Type.
  2. In the mobile app, click Ver items on a specific package.
  3. Result: The items list MUST hide all products not belonging to that package.
  4. Click Mostrar todos.
  5. Result: The full list MUST be restored.

Scenario 5: Proof of Delivery (Evidence)

  1. Go to Finalize for a delivery that requires a Signature and Photo (configurable in Delivery Status).
  2. Try to submit without evidence.
  3. Result: Form validation MUST prevent submission.
  4. Capture signature and photo, then submit.
  5. Result: The Picking in Odoo MUST be updated to the new status and include the Signature and Photo as attachments or fields.

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Common Resolutions

The Camera Scanner won't open?

Reason: Your browser may have blocked camera permissions for the site.

Fix: Go to your browser Site Settings, find your Odoo URL, and set Camera to "Allow". Reload the page.

Missing assignments in the list?

Reason: You might be looking at the History tab or need a refresh.

Fix: Toggle to the To Do tab and use the Pull-to-Refresh gesture from the top of the screen.

"Ver items" button is missing?

Reason: The visibility depends on the Picking Type configuration.

Fix: Ask your administrator to enable Show Items in Portal Delivery in the Inventory Operation Type settings.

Does it work without internet?

Answer: Partial Offline Support.

Detail: If you lose connection during a delivery, the PWA will keep the page active. However, Sync (submitting evidence) requires a data connection (3G/4G/WiFi).
