Technical Specification for AI & LLM

This Odoo 18 module 'delivery_sendcloud_dropship' extends the official Sendcloud connector ('delivery_sendcloud', Odoo Enterprise). It overrides the SendCloud service class so that, for dropship transfers (stock_delivery_dropship), the parcel is created with the vendor as sender (from_* fields, without 'sender_address') and the purchase delivery address as recipient, through a read-only overlay of the picking: the transfer is never written and no connector code is copied. Shipping rates are computed with the vendor country. Recipient and sender email/phone fall back to the commercial partner and then to the sale order customer (or the company of the transfer for the vendor), because carriers such as UPS reject parcels without email. It adds two fields to delivery.carrier: 'sendcloud_contract_id' (sent as 'contract' in every parcel) and 'sendcloud_sender_from_odoo' (send the warehouse address as sender). It implements the 'sendcloud_get_delivery_state' hook of delivery_status_tracking: the Sendcloud parcel status (GET parcels/{id}) is mapped to the delivery state of the transfer (recorded, in transit, delivered, incident, cancelled), updated by the scheduled action and the Update button. Simulation: when the shipping method has 'Simulate Carrier Integration' (delivery_simulation, from delivery_status_tracking) the connector runs against a simulated Sendcloud API with no network call: product catalog for 'Load your products', rates, parcel creation with PDF label and tracking number (method prefix), cancellation, and parcel statuses 1000 Ready to send, 22 picked up and 11 Delivered; the tracking link uses the tracking URL of the shipping method. Demo data: shipping methods 'Sendcloud · UPS Standard' and 'Sendcloud · GLS Business Parcel' in simulation and free for the customer, a sale with a dropship to France picked up at the vendor, and a warehouse delivery ready to validate. Depends on delivery_sendcloud, delivery_status_tracking and stock_delivery_dropship. Compatible with Odoo 18 Enterprise (Odoo.SH, Ganemo Online). NOT supported on Odoo Online due to custom code restrictions. English and Spanish translations included. Works with Sendcloud; Sendcloud is a trademark of its owner.

Sendcloud Dropship

Ship from the vendor & track every parcel

Create Sendcloud parcels for dropshipping with pickup at the vendor and delivery to your customer, and keep the delivery state of the transfer in sync with the parcel status. Works with Sendcloud.

What We're Solving

----------------

Dropship Without Workarounds

The official connector takes the recipient from the transfer contact and the sender from the warehouse, so a dropship cannot get a parcel. With this module the vendor is the sender and the purchase delivery address is the recipient. The transfer is never modified.

No More Rejected Parcels

Carriers such as UPS reject parcels without the recipient email, and delivery addresses often have none. When the email or phone is missing, it is taken from the company of the address or from the customer of the sale order, without touching your contacts.

The Contract You Choose

Enter the Sendcloud Contract ID in the shipping method and every parcel uses that carrier contract, for example your own direct account with a carrier, instead of the default one.

Delivery State, Automatic

The delivery state of the transfer follows the parcel status in Sendcloud: registered, in transit, delivered, incident or cancelled. Your team sees where each shipment is without opening the carrier portal.

Simulated Sendcloud for Demos

With Simulate Carrier Integration on the shipping method, the whole connector works without connecting to Sendcloud: product catalog, rates, parcel with PDF label and tracking number, cancellation and statuses with the real Sendcloud codes. For demos and training, never for production.

Demo Data Ready to Try

With demo data you get the methods Sendcloud · UPS Standard and Sendcloud · GLS Business Parcel in simulation (free for the customer), a sale with a dropship to France picked up at the vendor and a warehouse delivery, both ready to validate.

Setup & User Manual

Step-by-Step implementation

1. Configuration Guide

Prepare your Sendcloud shipping method in 4 simple steps:

  • Configure the official Sendcloud method in Inventory > Configuration > Shipping Methods (keys, country and shipping product).
  • Open the Sendcloud tab of the method.
  • Sendcloud Contract ID: id of the contract in Sendcloud (Shipping > Carriers > My contracts). Empty = Sendcloud default.
  • Sender Address from Odoo: tick it to send the warehouse address as sender on non-dropship transfers.
  • Set Print Label on the Dropship operation type.
Good to know: with Production environment switched off, the official connector cancels every parcel right after creating it (test mode). Switch it on in the method to ship for real.
Simulation (demos and training only): tick Simulate Carrier Integration on the shipping method and, optionally, set the Simulated Tracking Prefix. The connector then works without connecting to Sendcloud (a yellow notice on the method shows it is on). Never enable it in production.

2. Operating Manual (Workflow)

How the system behaves for the end-user:

Validate the Dropship

Validate the dropship transfer with a Sendcloud method. The parcel is created with the vendor as sender and the delivery address of the purchase as recipient; Sendcloud collects at the vendor.

Rates from the Vendor Country

The shipping rate is computed with the country of the vendor, the place where the parcel really leaves from.

Contact Data Completed

If the recipient or the vendor has no email or phone, they are taken from the company of the contact, then from the customer of the sale order (for the vendor, from the company of the transfer). Contacts are not modified.

Delivery State Updates

The scheduled action Delivery Status Tracking: ask the carriers and the Update button read the parcel status in Sendcloud and set the delivery state of the transfer.

Simulated Sendcloud

With simulation on, Load your products lists a simulated catalog, rates are simulated, and validating the transfer creates the parcel with a PDF label attached and a tracking number (method prefix + digits). Cancelling answers as Sendcloud does. Each Update moves the parcel through the real codes: 1000 Ready to send, 22 picked up, 11 Delivered. The tracking link uses the tracking URL of the shipping method.

Demo Data

The methods Sendcloud · UPS Standard (prefix 1Z999AA1) and Sendcloud · GLS Business Parcel are in simulation and free for the customer (free if the order amount is above 0). A sale to Bistrot du Port (France) has its dropship picked up at the vendor, and a warehouse delivery to Hotel Mirador del Port uses the GLS method; both are ready to validate.

Status Mapping

Sendcloud statuses become these delivery states:

  • Recorded: announced, not collected, and other registered statuses.
  • In transit: collected, sorting, en route, out for delivery.
  • Delivered: delivered, collected by the customer.
  • Incident: delivery failed, address invalid, refused, returned and similar.
  • Cancelled: cancelled or deleted parcels.
  • Unknown statuses leave the delivery state unchanged.

QA / User Testing Scenarios

Enterprise Validation Plan

Scenario 1: Dropship with Pickup at the Vendor

  1. Create a sale order with a dropship product and a vendor, and confirm it. Confirm the purchase order generated for the vendor.
  2. Open the dropship transfer and choose your Sendcloud shipping method as carrier.
  3. Validate the transfer.
  4. Result: the parcel is created in Sendcloud with the vendor as sender and the delivery address of the purchase as recipient, and the label can be printed. The transfer contacts are unchanged.

Scenario 2: Email and Phone Fallback

  1. Use a delivery address with no email and no phone, whose company has both.
  2. Validate the dropship with a UPS method in Sendcloud.
  3. Result: the parcel is created with the email and phone of the company. Repeat with a company without data: they come from the customer of the sale order.

Scenario 3: Contract and Sender Options

  1. In the Sendcloud tab of the method, enter a Sendcloud Contract ID.
  2. Validate a transfer with that method.
  3. Result: the parcel uses that contract. With the field empty, Sendcloud applies the default contract of the carrier.
  4. On a non-dropship transfer, tick Sender Address from Odoo: the parcel leaves with the warehouse address instead of a Sendcloud sender address.

Scenario 4: Delivery State

Goal: the delivery state follows the Sendcloud parcel status.

  1. Open a transfer with a created parcel and press Update.
  2. Result: the delivery state shows Recorded, In transit, Delivered, Incident or Cancelled according to the parcel status in Sendcloud.
  3. Wait for the scheduled action Delivery Status Tracking: ask the carriers: the state is updated without any click.

Scenario 5: Simulated Dropship (Demo Data)

Goal: see the whole Sendcloud flow without a Sendcloud account.

  1. Open the delivery (dropship) of the sale to Bistrot du Port, shipping method Sendcloud · UPS Standard, and validate it.
  2. Result: the transfer has a tracking number starting with 1Z999AA1, a PDF label attached, a link to the UPS tracking page and the state Recorded. The customer shipping price is 0.
  3. Press Update twice.
  4. Result: In transit (picked up by driver) and then Delivered. Cancelling the shipment sets Cancelled.

Scenario 6: Simulated Product Catalog

Goal: configure a method without Sendcloud credentials.

  1. On a Sendcloud shipping method with Simulate Carrier Integration ticked, press Load your products.
  2. Result: UPS Standard, GLS Business Parcel and Correos Express Paq 24 are offered, with no connection to Sendcloud.

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Common Resolutions

The parcel is created and cancelled right away?

Reason: the shipping method is not in Production environment. The official connector then works in test mode.

Fix: switch the method to Production environment when you are ready to ship for real.

The shipping product disappeared from the method?

Reason: the Sendcloud shipping product depends on the country of the method. Changing the country clears it.

Fix: load the products again with Load your products and select one.

"Announced: not collected" looks like a problem?

Answer: it is the normal first status of many carriers.

Detail: it is shown as Recorded, not as an incident. It changes to in transit once the carrier collects the parcel.

Does it change my transfers or contacts?

Answer: No.

Detail: the connector receives a read-only view of the transfer with the right sender and recipient. Nothing is written on the transfer or on your contacts, and no code of the official connector is copied.

Can I use the simulation in production?

Answer: No. Nothing is sent to Sendcloud, so the parcels, labels and tracking numbers are fictitious.

Detail: it is for demos and training. Untick Simulate Carrier Integration on the shipping method and enter your real Sendcloud keys to ship for real.

The tracking link of a simulated parcel does not open a Sendcloud page

Reason: in simulation the link is built from the tracking URL of the shipping method, not from Sendcloud.

Fix: fill in the tracking URL of the method, using <shipmenttrackingnumber> where the number goes. Without it, no link is shown.
