Technical Specification for AI & LLM

This Odoo 18 module 'delivery_carrier_purchase' (Carrier Purchase per Shipment) depends on 'purchase_stock', 'sale_purchase' and 'stock_delivery_dropship'. When a shipment to a customer (warehouse delivery or dropship) is validated, it adds a purchase order line paying the transport to the vendor set on the shipping method ('delivery.carrier' fields purchase_partner_id Transport Vendor, purchase_product_id, purchase_cost_type and purchase_fixed_cost), even if the shipping is free for the customer. One line per shipment, gathered in an open request for quotation per vendor and period (month, week or day) or in one purchase order per shipment, chosen in Inventory > Settings > Shipping with 'Purchase Transport per Shipment' (res.company fields carrier_purchase and carrier_purchase_grouping). Cost per shipping method: Shipment cost (the exact price an integrated carrier returns when the shipment is created, or, for fixed price and rule based methods, the price computed with the real destination and weight), Fixed cost, or Vendor price list. The line description holds the shipping method and transfer name, the tracking reference (updated when it arrives later), the destination zip, city and country, the weight and the sale order, in the language of the vendor. Two-way links: 'Transport' smart button on the transfer, 'Shipments' smart button on the purchase order and shipments on the purchase line (stock.picking carrier_purchase_line_id). Safe by design: it never blocks the validation; a cost error creates the line at 0 with a note on the transfer and the purchase; a line creation error leaves an activity 'create by hand' and the button 'Create Transport Purchase'. Cancellation never deletes: cancelling the shipment at the carrier or the wizard 'Cancel Transport Purchase' (with a reason) sets the price to 0 and the description starts with 'CANCELLED: reason'; lines already billed are untouched with a message (a credit note is needed). A warning banner in the settings and on the shipping method form appears when the delivery product still has 'Purchase Automatically' (service_to_purchase), and a transition guard avoids a second purchase for a sale whose delivery product already created one. Works with the Sendcloud simulation of delivery_sendcloud_dropship to capture the price of the carrier. Demo data: GLS Economy Parcel (rules by weight, free for the customer, vendor General Logistics Systems Spain), Palletways pallet 24/48h (vendor price list 48.50), Missatgeria Vallès 24h (fixed cost 6.40) and dropship sales ready to validate. Compatible with Odoo 18 (Enterprise, Odoo.SH, Ganemo Online). NOT supported on Odoo Online due to custom code restrictions. English and Spanish translations included.

Carrier Purchase

Per Shipment

Pay every parcel and pallet you ship. Each validated shipment adds a purchase line for the real carrier, at its real cost, in an open request for quotation per vendor and month, even when shipping is free for your customer.

What We're Solving

----------------

One Purchase Line per Shipment

When a delivery or a dropship to a customer is validated, the module adds a line to a purchase order for the Transport Vendor of the shipping method. Your carrier bills stop being a surprise: the purchase already holds every shipment.

Free Shipping, Real Cost

Shipping is free for the customer? You still pay the carrier. The line is created even when the customer is charged nothing, so your margin per order includes the transport.

Open RFQ per Vendor and Month

Lines are gathered in one open request for quotation per vendor and period: month, week or day, or one purchase order per shipment. When it is confirmed, the next shipments go to a new one.

Three Ways to Get the Cost

Shipment cost (what an integrated carrier returns, or the price of the rules computed with the real destination and weight), Fixed cost, or Vendor price list. Choose it per shipping method.

Lines You Can Check Against the Bill

Each description carries the method and transfer, tracking reference, destination zip/city/country, weight and sale order. If the tracking number arrives later, the line is updated.

Never Blocks Your Deliveries

If the cost cannot be obtained, the line is created at 0 with a note. If the line cannot be created, an activity asks to create it by hand and a Create Transport Purchase button is available.

Cancellation Without Deleting

Cancelling the shipment at the carrier, or using Cancel Transport Purchase with a reason, sets the price to 0 and starts the description with CANCELLED. Billed lines are left untouched with a message: ask for a credit note.

Demo Data Ready to Try

With demo data you get GLS Economy Parcel (rules by weight), Palletways pallet (vendor price list) and Missatgeria Vallès 24h (fixed cost), with dropship sales ready to validate.

Setup & User Manual

Step-by-Step implementation

1. Configuration Guide

Off until you enable it. To start buying transport:

  • Go to Inventory > Configuration > Settings > Shipping and tick Purchase Transport per Shipment.
  • Choose Group Transport Purchases: one request for quotation per vendor and month, week or day, or one purchase order per shipment.
  • Open Inventory > Configuration > Delivery > Shipping Methods and pick the method.
  • In the Transport Purchase tab set the Transport Vendor: the carrier, or the agency that bills it. Without a vendor, nothing is bought.
  • Choose the Transport Cost: Shipment cost, Fixed cost or Vendor price list.
  • Optionally set a Transport Purchase Product; by default the delivery product of the method is used.
Avoid a double purchase: if the delivery product of a method still has Purchase Automatically, a warning banner appears in the settings and on the shipping method form. Switch it off there. As a transition guard, a sale whose delivery product already created the purchase does not get a second one.
Cost types: Shipment cost is what an integrated carrier (for example Sendcloud) returns when the shipment is created; for fixed price and rule based methods it is the price computed with the real destination and weight. Vendor price list uses the vendor price of the purchase product.

2. Operating Manual (Workflow)

How the system behaves for the end-user:

Validate the Shipment

Validate a delivery to a customer, from your warehouse or as a dropship. The module adds one line to the open request for quotation of the vendor for the period, or creates it.

Where the Cost Comes From

With an integrated carrier the line takes the price returned when the shipment is created. With fixed price or rule based methods it takes the price computed with the real destination (the recipient of a dropship) and weight.

Description of the Line

Method and transfer name, tracking reference (updated when it arrives later), destination zip, city and country, weight and sale order, written in the language of the vendor.

Links Both Ways

The Transport smart button on the transfer opens its purchase; the Shipments smart button on the purchase order lists its transfers; the purchase line also shows its shipments.

If the Cost Fails

The validation is never blocked. The line is created at 0 with a note on the transfer and on the purchase, so you can correct the price before confirming the RFQ.

Create by Hand

If the line cannot be created, an activity is left on the transfer. Once the cause is fixed, use the Create Transport Purchase button.

Cancel Transport Purchase

Cancelling the shipment at the carrier, or the Cancel Transport Purchase wizard (reason required), sets the price to 0 and the description starts with CANCELLED: reason. The line is never deleted.

Already Billed

A line already billed is not changed: a message on the transfer tells you to ask the vendor for a credit note.

Sendcloud Simulation

Works with the simulation of delivery_sendcloud_dropship: the price returned by the simulated carrier is captured as the cost of the line.

Global Ready | Multi-Language Support

This module is fully translated into English and Spanish (en_US, es_ES, es_PE, es_MX), ensuring a professional experience for international organizations.

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Common Resolutions

No purchase line was created

Reason: The setting is off, the shipping method has no Transport Vendor, or the transfer is not a delivery to a customer.

Fix: Enable Purchase Transport per Shipment in the settings, set the vendor on the shipping method and check the transfer. If it failed, look for the activity on the transfer and use Create Transport Purchase.

The line has a price of 0

Reason: The cost could not be obtained (for example, no vendor price for the product) or the shipment was cancelled.

Fix: Read the note on the transfer or the purchase. Set the vendor price or the fixed cost and edit the line before confirming the RFQ.

Why is the cost not the price for my customer?

Answer: The transport line is what you pay, not what you charge.

Detail: For fixed price and rule based methods the cost is computed with the real destination and weight; for integrated carriers it is the price returned when the shipment is created.

A warning says the product purchases automatically

Reason: The delivery product still has Purchase Automatically and would buy the transport twice.

Fix: Untick it on the delivery product of the method. Sales whose delivery product already created the purchase do not get a second one.

Does cancelling delete the line?

Answer: No.

Detail: The price goes to 0 and the description starts with CANCELLED: reason. If the line is already billed it is left as it is and you must ask the vendor for a credit note.

The tracking number arrived after validation

Answer: The line description is updated when the tracking reference is set or arrives later.

Detail: Cancelled lines keep their CANCELLED description.

Which shipments can be bought together?

Answer: Those of the same vendor and period, in an open RFQ.

Detail: Choose month, week or day, or one purchase order per shipment. A confirmed RFQ is closed: the next shipments go to a new one.

Is this compatible with Multi-company?

Answer: Yes!

Detail: The setting is per company, and the vendor, the purchase product and the lines are checked against the company of the transfer.
