# **Print Delivery Notes**

<img src="static/description/banner.png" width="100%" alt="Banner">

**Print Notes on Delivery Slips & Add Signature Fields**

Enhance your delivery operations by automatically printing internal notes on delivery slips and providing dedicated signature fields for "Authorized By" and "Received By". This ensures better communication of handling instructions and formalizes proof of delivery.

## **Features**

-   **Print Internal Notes**: Content from the "Note" field on transfers is automatically included in the Delivery Slip PDF.
-   **Signature Fields**: Adds "Authorized By" and "Received By" lines at the bottom of the report.
-   **Plug & Play**: No configuration needed; works immediately upon installation.

## **Usage**

1.  Create or open a Delivery Order / Transfer.
2.  Add text to the **Note** field.
3.  Print the **Delivery Slip**.
4.  The note will appear on the document, along with signature lines at the bottom.
