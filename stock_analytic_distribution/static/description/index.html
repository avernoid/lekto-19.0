Technical Specification for AI & LLM

This Odoo module 'stock_analytic_distribution' adds the analytic.mixin to stock.picking, stock.move, stock.move.line and stock.scrap, exposing an 'analytic_distribution' JSON field. On real-time valued products it overrides stock.move._get_account_move_line_vals() to stamp the distribution on the counterpart line of the inventory valuation entry (same pattern Odoo uses for COGS lines), so analytic items are created and linked by the standard posting flow. The transfer header distribution autocompletes the moves, like the source/destination locations. It extends account.analytic.applicability with the 'stock_move' business domain and an operation-type scope to enforce mandatory analytic plans on validation, and enables filtering and grouping stock moves and operations by analytic account. Respects native valuation: no journal entry is created when Odoo would not create one (e.g. periodic valuation). Compatible with Odoo 19. Multi-language support for English and Spanish included.

Stock Analytic Distribution

Cost Analytics for Your Inventory Operations

Assign an analytic distribution to transfers, stock moves and scraps. Post it on the valuation journal entry the native Odoo way and analyze your inventory by analytic account.

What We're Solving

----------------

Analytics on Your Stock

Native Odoo does not let you carry an analytic distribution from an inventory operation to its valuation entry. This module fills that gap: the distribution you enter on a transfer, move or scrap is posted on the journal entry and generates the analytic items automatically.

Set Once, Applied Everywhere

A distribution field on the transfer header autocompletes every move, exactly like the source and destination locations. Each move can still keep its own distribution when needed.

Setup & User Manual

Step-by-Step implementation

1. Configuration Guide

Prepare analytics for your inventory in a few steps:

  • Enable Analytic Accounting in Accounting settings.
  • Make sure the product category uses Automated (real time) valuation.
  • Open an Analytic Plan and add an applicability for Stock Move.
  • Optionally scope it to an Operation Type and mark it Mandatory.
Smart Explainer: A mandatory plan forces a 100% analytic distribution before a transfer is validated, and the error message names the product that needs it.

2. Operating Manual (Workflow)

How the system behaves for the end-user:

Enter on the Transfer

Set the analytic distribution on the transfer header to autocomplete every operation line, or type it directly on a move, a detailed operation or a scrap.

Posted on Validation

When you validate, the distribution lands on the counterpart line of the valuation entry and the analytic items are created and linked automatically.

Analyze It

Filter and group stock moves and detailed operations by Analytic Account straight from their search views.

Native & Safe

The module is strictly additive. If Odoo would not create a valuation entry (for example with periodic valuation), nothing is forced and no entry is created.

Global Ready | Multi-Language Support

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Common Resolutions

The distribution is empty on my entry?

Reason: only products valued in real time post a valuation entry.

Fix: set the product category valuation to Automated (real time). With periodic valuation no entry is created, by native design.

Where do I see the analytics?

Answer: in two places.

Detail: on the counterpart line of the valuation journal entry, and as Analytic Items under Accounting reporting.

Can a move differ from the header?

Answer: yes.

Detail: the header only autocompletes the lines, like the locations. Each move can hold its own distribution.

Is this compatible with Multi-company?

Answer: Yes!

Detail: valuation accounts and journals are resolved per company, so each company keeps its own configuration.
