AI & Intelligence Hub

This module, first_and_last_name, is designed to enhance the Odoo Partner model (res.partner) by desegregating full names into distinct components: partner_name (First Name), first_name (Paternal Last Name), and second_name (Maternal Last Name). This architectural choice facilitates superior data mining, precise tax reporting (SUNAT, SAT, AEAT), and compliance with European and Latin American identification standards.
