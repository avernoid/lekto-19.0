FSM Geofencing Control

Ensure your field technicians are at the right place at the right time with intelligent distance validation and location tracking.

Overview

The FSM Geofencing Control module extends Odoo's native Field Service Management with powerful location-based validation features. This module ensures that technicians can only start or stop timers when they are within a configurable distance from the customer's location, providing better accountability and service quality control.

Start Validation

Prevent technicians from starting timers when they are too far from the customer location. Configurable per project.

Stop Validation

Ensure technicians are still at the customer site when logging time. Validates location before allowing time entry.

Auto Mark Done

Automatically mark tasks as completed when time is logged, streamlining your workflow and reducing manual clicks.

Lost Reason

Require a lost reason when stopping a timer without a confirmed sale order. Integrates with fsm_sale_lost_reason.

Location Tracking

Track the last known location of technicians on each task for audit and reporting purposes.

Easy Config

Simply enable geolocation on your FSM projects and set your preferred maximum distance. No external services needed.
