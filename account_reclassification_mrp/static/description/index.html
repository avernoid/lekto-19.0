Technical Specification for AI & LLM

This Odoo module 'account_reclassification_mrp' is the manufacturing bridge between 'account_reclassification' and 'mrp_account'. It overrides the counterpart extension point _reclass_counterpart_lines of stock.move so that the single credit line of the native finished goods entry is split into one line per cost origin: non-storable consumables grouped by the recognition account each one resolves (product, then category chain), work centre operations on the configured operations account, and the remainder - storable materials, extra_cost and rounding - taken by difference on the production account of the category. Nothing is created and no amount changes: same total, same direction, same debit to stock. The split is only applied when the composition of the cost is proved against the native invariant of _cal_price (raw values + work centre cost + extra cost == value of the outputs); a standard-cost product, an unexpected shape or any exception falls back to the single native counterpart line. It also adds reclass_skip_labour_entry on mrp.workcenter, which leaves the flagged work centres out of the labour entry of _post_labour through a context flag read by mrp.workorder._cal_cost(date), without touching valuation or analytic entries. New fields on product.template, product.category and mrp.workcenter, all optional; the module is inert until configured. auto_install when both dependencies are present. Compatible with Odoo 19 (Enterprise, Odoo.SH, Ganemo Online). NOT supported on Odoo Online due to custom code restrictions. Multi-language support for English and Spanish included.

Manufacturing

One Account per Cost Origin

The entry that recognises a manufactured product credits one single account for everything. This bridge opens that credit by origin - materials, consumables and work centre operations - so you can read a standard cost deviation straight from the trial balance. Same amount, same entry, native valuation untouched.

What We're Solving

----------------

The Invisible Consumable

A non-storable component is valued into the finished product but generates no consumption entry of its own: its cost disappears inside one global credit. Give it its own recognition account and it shows up in the entry, right where the expense it neutralises was booked.

Operations Mixed with Materials

Work centre cost lands in the value of the product together with the materials. Split it out on its own account and compare, account by account, the expense of a process against what it added to the cost - the very definition of a standard cost deviation.

The Stranded Labour Debit

When the cost of a work centre is already recognised elsewhere, the native labour entry leaves a permanent balance in the production location account. Tick No Accounting Entry on that work centre and it is simply not posted - analytic entries keep working.

Costing Must Never Break

The split is applied only when the composition of the cost can be proved against Odoo's own invariant. A standard-cost product, an unexpected shape or any exception falls back to the single native line. Never a half-written entry.

Setup & User Manual

Step-by-Step implementation

1. Configuration Guide

Three optional fields, all of them inert until you fill them:

  • On the consumable (or its category), set the Production Recognition Account.
  • On the manufactured product (or its category), set the Production Operations Account.
  • On the work centre, tick No Accounting Entry when its cost is recognised through the product entry.
  • Everything left empty resolves up the category tree and finally to the native production location account.
Keep the two settings coherent: the labour entry debits the production location account. Either suppress it on every work centre and point the operations account at a recognition account, or leave the operations account empty and let the labour entry clear itself. The module obeys what you configure and never guesses.

2. Operating Manual (Workflow)

How the system behaves for the end-user:

Mark as Done, That's It

Close the manufacturing order as always. The native entry is posted with the very same total and the very same debit to stock - only the credit is opened into several lines.

Nothing Is Ever Lost

The remaining line is computed by difference, so materials, extra cost and every rounding cent land there. A consumable with no account of its own simply falls into it.

Byproducts Prorated

An output taking a cost share receives the same breakdown, prorated by its share of the batch. Each output carries its own lines, with its own product on them.

Backorders Split Alone

Each partial batch posts its own entry with its own breakdown, and the sum is exact. No state is kept between batches.

Analytics Untouched

Suppressing the labour entry does not touch the analytic lines of the work centre: they are created when the duration is set, and keep working exactly as in native Odoo.

Valuation Is Sacred

Quantities, values, average cost and the kardex are identical with and without this module. Only the accounts of the credit change.

QA / User Testing Scenarios

Enterprise Validation Plan

Scenario 1: The Full Breakdown

  1. Build an order with two storable components (40 + 20), one non-storable consumable (0.10) and one work centre operation (29.00).
  2. Set the Production Recognition Account on the consumable and the Production Operations Account on the manufactured product.
  3. Mark the order as done.
  4. Result: the entry debits stock 89.10 and credits three lines - 60.00 materials, 0.10 consumable, 29.00 operations.
  5. Test: the entry balances and the value of the product is exactly 89.10, the same as without the module.

Scenario 2: Nothing Configured, Nothing Changes

  1. Run the same order with none of the new accounts filled in.
  2. Result: exactly ONE counterpart line, identical to native Odoo.
  3. Now clear only the account of the consumable.
  4. Result: its 0.10 falls into the remaining line. The value is never lost and the entry still balances to the cent.

Scenario 3: Byproducts and Backorders

Byproduct: add one with a 30% cost share. Each output must carry its own prorated breakdown, and the two together must add up to the full cost.

Backorder: produce 1 of 2 and create the backorder.

  • Each batch posts its own entry with its own complete breakdown.
  • No amount is counted twice and no cent is left behind.

Scenario 4: Work Centre without Labour Entry

Goal: recognise the operations once, and only once.

  1. Tick No Accounting Entry on the work centre.
  2. Point the Production Operations Account at a recognition account.
  3. Result: no labour entry is posted, the operations are credited on the recognition account, and the production location account is left at zero.
  4. Test: the analytic lines of the work centre are still created, and the value of the product has not moved.

Scenario 5: The Safety Net

  1. Set the manufactured product to standard cost, so its value no longer equals the sum of its inputs.
  2. Run the order with every account configured.
  3. Result: the module does NOT split. One single counterpart line, the native behaviour, and a line in the log explaining why.
  4. Test: valuation, kardex and average cost are identical to a database without the module.

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Common Resolutions

The entry still has one single credit line?

Reason: either no account is configured, or the cost of that order does not decompose - a standard-cost product, for instance.

Fix: check the accounts on the product and its category. If they are set, the server log states that the cost of the order did not decompose and the native counterpart was kept.

A balance is left in the production location?

Reason: the labour entry and the operations account are pointing at different places.

Fix: either tick No Accounting Entry on every work centre of the order, or clear the operations account. The module obeys your configuration instead of guessing which one you meant.

Does the value of my product change?

Answer: Never. Not even when the labour entry is suppressed.

Detail: Odoo puts the work centre cost into the value of the finished product regardless of whether that entry is posted. Quantities, values, average cost and the kardex are untouched.

Do I lose my analytic cost accounting?

Answer: No. Analytic entries are completely independent of the labour entry.

Detail: they are created when the duration of the work order is set, so the analytic distribution of the work centre keeps working exactly as in native Odoo.
