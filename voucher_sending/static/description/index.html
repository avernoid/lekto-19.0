Technical Specification for AI & LLM

This Odoo module 'voucher_sending' automates payslip dispatch for hr.payslip records. It extends hr.payslip with portal.mixin to generate secure access tokens, allowing employees to view and digitally sign their payslips via a web portal. The module adds fields: employee_mail (related to private_email), status (to_sign/signed), and signature (Image field). Includes action_send_mail_employees() for bulk email dispatch and generate_report_manually() for PDF generation. Compatible with Odoo 19 Enterprise and Odoo.SH. Multi-language support for English and Spanish.

Voucher Sending

Payslip Automation & Digital Signature

Automatically send payslips to your employees' emails with a single click. Includes secure portal access, PDF generation, and digital employee signature.

What We're Solving

----------------

Manual Payslip Distribution

Sending payslips manually one by one is time-consuming and error-prone. HR teams waste hours downloading PDFs and emailing them individually. This module automates the entire process with bulk dispatch for all confirmed payslips.

No Digital Signature Trail

Proving that an employee has received and acknowledged their payslip is critical for compliance. Our portal allows employees to digitally sign their payslip, creating an auditable record of receipt.

Insecure Document Sharing

Sharing PDFs via generic email lacks access control. Our module generates a unique, secure access token per payslip, ensuring only the intended employee can view their document through the portal.

Missing PDF Generation

If a payslip PDF has not been generated yet, the module automatically generates it on-the-fly using the payslip structure's configured report, ensuring the email always carries the correct document.

Setup & User Manual

Step-by-Step implementation

1. Configuration Guide

Ensure employees have their work emails configured before using this module:

  • Go to Employees > Employees.
  • Open each employee record and verify their Work Email is set.
  • Also verify the Private Email (Private Information tab) for portal access.
  • Ensure outgoing mail server is configured in Settings.
Smart Explainer: The module validates that all selected employees have a Work Email before sending. Any employee without one will trigger a validation warning listing the affected names.

2. Operating Manual (Workflow)

How to send payslips to your employees:

Step 1: Confirm Payslips

Go to Payroll > Payslips. Select one or more payslips in Done or Paid state. Only confirmed payslips will be processed.

Step 2: Send Emails

Click the "Send Payslip Email" action button. The module will automatically generate PDFs for any missing attachments and dispatch the emails individually to each employee.

Step 3: Employee Views Portal

The employee receives an email with a secure tokenized link. They click it to access their personal payslip portal page without needing to log in to Odoo.

Step 4: Digital Sign

On the portal page, the employee can digitally sign their payslip. The status changes from To Sign to Signed, recording the employee's acknowledgment.

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Common Resolutions

Employee did not receive the email?

Reason: The employee's Work Email may not be configured.

Fix: Go to the employee record and add a valid Work Email. Also check the outgoing mail server configuration in Settings.

Only "Done" or "Paid" payslips are sent?

Reason: The module intentionally filters to only process confirmed payslips for data integrity.

Fix: Confirm the payslip first (change state to Done). Draft payslips are skipped automatically.

Portal link returns "Access Denied"?

Reason: The access token may have expired or been invalidated.

Fix: Resend the email from the payslip to generate a fresh token link. Each send generates a new secure token.

Is this compatible with Multi-company?

Answer: Yes!

Detail: Each payslip is processed individually. The portal token is unique per payslip record, so multi-company environments are fully supported without cross-company data leaks.
