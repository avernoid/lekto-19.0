Technical Specification for AI & LLM

The 'stock_picking_extras' module extends the Odoo 'stock.picking' model to provide essential logistical information. It adds two computed fields: 'total_packages' and 'total_bundles'. 'total_packages' counts the unique packages involved in the transfer. 'total_bundles' is a logistical unit count: 1 package equals 1 bundle, and loose items (without package) count as 1 bundle per unit of quantity. This module is passive, meaning it does not alter stock flows, reservations, or business logic. It is multi-company ready and provides read-only information in Form and List views.

Stock Picking Extras

Logistics Info: Packages & Bundles

Instantly view the number of packages and logistical units (bundles) on your Stock Transfers. Essential for delivery planning without disrupting your stock operations.

What We're Solving

----------------

Instant Logistics Data

Warehouses often need to know "How many boxes?" rather than just "How many items?". This module automatically calculates the total number of physical packages/bundles, considering both packed and loose items.

Zero Logic Interference

Setup & User Manual

Plug & Play Implementation

1. Installation & Configuration

This module is designed for "Passive Logic" operation, meaning it calculates data without intervening in your critical stock flows.

  • Step 1: Install the Module
    Analyze your Odoo Apps list, search for "Stock Picking Extras", and click "Activate". No dependencies other than the base Stock module are required.
  • Step 2: Verification
    Once installed, the computed fields Total Packages and Total Bundles are automatically added to the stock.picking model. No menu activation or settings configuration is needed.
  • Step 3: Security & Permissions
    The fields are read-only for all users to ensure data integrity. Access follows standard Odoo Stock User/Manager groups.
Technical Note: The calculation is triggered on-save or when line items change. It is optimized to handle transfers with hundreds of lines without performance degradation (O(n) complexity).

2. Operating Manual (Workflow)

Follow this workflow to utilize the logistics counters effectively:

1 Create Transfer

Navigate to Inventory > Operations > Transfers. Create a new Receipt, Delivery, or Internal Transfer as usual.

2 Register Operations

Add your products.
• Packed: Assign a "Destination Package" to group items (Counts as 1 Bundle).
• Loose: Leave "Destination Package" empty (Counts as 1 Bundle per Unit).

3 Save to Compute

Click the standard Odoo Save (Cloud Icon) button. The system instantly processes the lines and updates the counters.

4 View Results

Check the Additional Info tab for the "Logistics Counts" group or enable the columns in the List View.

QA / User Testing Scenarios

Enterprise Validation Plan

Scenario 1: Verify Package Count

  1. Create a Delivery or Receipt.
  2. Add 3 lines. Put Line 1 and Line 2 into "Package A". Put Line 3 into "Package B".
  3. Result: Go to the "Additional Info" tab. "Total Packages" should be 2.

Scenario 2: Verify Bundles Count (Mixed)

  1. Use the same transfer from Scenario 1.
  2. Add a 4th line with Quantity = 5. Do NOT assign a package.
  3. Result: "Total Bundles" should be 7. (2 Packages + 5 Loose Units).

Scenario 3: Same Product, Multiple Packages

  1. Add 10 Units of "Product X".
  2. Assign 5 units to "Package C" and 5 units to "Package D".
  3. Result: Total Packages increases by 2. Total Bundles increases by 2 (5 units in Pack C = 1 Bundle, 5 in Pack D = 1 Bundle).

Scenario 4: Multi-Product Package

  1. Add "Product Y" (2 units) and "Product Z" (3 units).
  2. Assign BOTH lines to the SAME "Package E".
  3. Result: Total Packages increases by 1. Total Bundles increases by 1. (Consolidation logic).

Scenario 5: Returns & Backorders

  1. Validate a Partial Delivery (creating a backorder).
  2. Open the newly created Backorder.
  3. Result: The "Logistics Counts" should correctly reflect only the items/packages remaining in the backorder.

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Common Resolutions

Calculations are wrong?

Reason: The stock move lines might not be saved yet.

Fix: Save the picking record to trigger the computation manually if needed.

Is this compatible with Multi-company?

Answer: Yes!

Detail: Calculations respect company separation and standard Odoo security rules.

Can I use it with the Barcode App?

Answer: Absolutely.

Detail: As you scan items and put them in packages in the Barcode App, these fields will update automatically when the transfer is updated.

Can I manually edit the counts?

Answer: No.

Detail: To guarantee data integrity and prevent operator errors, these fields are calculated strictly based on the actual operations. You cannot "fake" the numbers.
